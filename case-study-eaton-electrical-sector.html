Client: Eaton

Energy Optimization Creative

Making the case for Eaton as the authority customers need to navigate the energy transition.

ClientEaton, Electrical Sector
CategoryLanding Pages & Banner Ads
Deliverable4 landing pages, 17 banner ads
RoleCopywriter
The brief

Selling authority, not just equipment

Eaton asked agency Jack Morton to create landing pages and banner ads speaking to the energy transition: creative that did more than sell hardware, built to show customers that Eaton understood the process and was best suited to help them through it.

The work

Four product stories, one banner line

Four Eaton solutions (grid resilience, energy storage, smart breakers, and the Eaton & Microsoft EnergyAware UPS) each got a landing page and a matching set of banner ads, tied together by a "Simplified means…" line that adapted to whatever the product actually did: keeping the power on, saving energy, seeing a grid.
