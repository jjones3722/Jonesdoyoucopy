Client: NBA

The NBA Cup Red Carpet

Launching the league's first-ever In-Season Tournament with a VIP red-carpet experience built from scratch.

ClientNBA (via Jack Morton Worldwide)
CategoryExperiential / Event Copy
LocationT-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas
RoleCopywriter, activation team

The Brief

The NBA needed a first-of-its-kind VIP experience to kick off its brand-new In-Season Tournament — a history-making moment for the league that required an entrance worthy of it.

The Approach

I was part of the team that won the business and helped concept the activation: choosing the venue, shaping the guest experience, and handling logistics and shifting needs until it was delivered — including the red-carpet copy and signage guests and press saw on arrival.

The Result

A golden-glamour red carpet experience at T-Mobile Arena that hosted NBA stars and cultural figures — including LeBron James and Timothée Chalamet — for a tournament that generated 1.1B+ impressions and 59M+ video views league-wide.

The copy

What actually ran on-site

Red carpet signage
"With the introduction of the new In-Season Tournament, this season is a history-making one for the NBA. And making history means making a grand entrance."
