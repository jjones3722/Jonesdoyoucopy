Client: NBA

The NBA Cup Red Carpet

Winning the pitch that launched the league's first-ever In-Season Tournament red carpet.

ClientNBA (via Jack Morton Worldwide)
CategoryNew-Business Pitch / Experiential
LocationT-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas
RolePitch lead, new-business team
01

The Brief

Before any red carpet could be built, Jack Morton had to win the assignment. The NBA's brand-new In-Season Tournament was a history-making opportunity — the agency needed a pitch that proved it was the right partner to deliver a first-of-its-kind live experience.

02

The Approach

I led the client pitch, grounding the presentation in Jack Morton's heritage, relevant experience, and differentiated approach to live brand experiences. I guided the NBA through activation concepts aligned with its business and audience goals — connecting the strategic opportunity to practical, high-impact ways the tournament could build fan engagement, cultural relevance, and long-term value. It was a collaborative new-business effort: I didn't write the final campaign copy, but I led the pitch narrative and presentation that helped the team articulate why Jack Morton was the right partner.

03

The Result

Jack Morton won the business and delivered a golden-glamour red carpet experience at T-Mobile Arena that hosted NBA stars and cultural figures — including LeBron James and Timothée Chalamet — for a tournament that generated 1.1B+ impressions and 59M+ video views league-wide.
