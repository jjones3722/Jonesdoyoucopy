Client: Ruiz HR Services

Speaking to the Leaders Who Actually Belong Here

A full website rewrite that swapped vague inspiration for language built to attract — and keep — the right clients.

ClientRuiz HR Services
CategoryWebsite Copywriting
DeliverableFull website copy rewrite
RoleCopywriter
The brief

Clear on the offer, unclear on the audience

Before this rewrite, Ruiz HR Services' site read fine — professional, polished, on-brand — but it wasn't aimed anywhere in particular. It was bringing in visitors who weren't actually the founder's target client. The site needed to do more than sound good; it needed to say, unmistakably, who it was for.

The approach

Writing for one reader, not everyone

I rebuilt the messaging around who founder Ariana Ruiz actually wants to work with: Directors of Operations and rising executives — many of them professionals of color — pushing toward VP-level roles. That meant naming the real tension her clients feel, being capable but not visible, present but not sponsored, instead of leaning on general leadership-coaching language. It's the thinking behind lines like "Visibility gets you noticed. Sponsorship gets you promoted."

The result

The right people started showing up

The site now filters for fit before a visitor ever books a call. It's translated into real business, too: the rewrite has led to repeat client relationships, not just one-off inquiries.
