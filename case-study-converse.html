Client: Converse

The Converse Awards

Copy for the merch, the signage, and the drinks in everyone's hand.

ClientConverse
CategoryBrand / Experiential
EventThe Converse Awards, 2023
RoleCopywriter — merch, signage, drink naming; activation concepting
01

The Brief

Converse needed copy that showed up everywhere attendees actually looked during its Converse Awards celebration — not one hero line, but a whole physical experience's worth of words: what was printed on the merch guests took home, what was posted around the room, and what was written on the drink menu in their hands.

02

The Approach

I wrote the copy for the tote bag merch, the event's signage, and named every drink on the cocktail menu — reworking classic cocktail names into Converse-flavored ones that nodded to the brand's sneaker heritage without hitting you over the head with it. I was also key in conceptualizing the final delivered activation — helping shape how the space and the copy worked together, not just filling in words after the concept was already locked.

03

The Result

The Converse Awards 2023 shipped as one consistent, on-voice experience — the tote line guests walked out with, the drink names they ordered at the bar, and the signage overhead all reading like they came from the same brand voice, because they did.
