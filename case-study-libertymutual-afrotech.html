Client: Liberty Mutual

'23 AFROTECH Conference

Liberty Mutual's first year sponsoring the largest gathering of Black tech professionals in the country — and a booth built to make it count.

ClientLiberty Mutual
CategoryRecruiting & Brand Activation
DeliverableBooth activation, email campaigns, social & on-site trivia game
RoleCopywriter
01

The Brief

2023 marked Liberty Mutual's first year sponsoring AFROTECH — the largest gathering of Black tech professionals in the country, with 20,000+ attendees. The booth needed to do three things at once: recruit top-tier, underrepresented tech talent, position Liberty as a genuine technology leader (not just an insurance brand showing up), and give Liberty's own employees at the event a reason to network and grow.

02

The Approach

I wrote the copy across every touchpoint that got someone to the booth and kept them there: pre- and post-event email campaigns, LinkedIn social content building anticipation and following up, a looping on-screen presentation running at the booth all conference long, and a trivia game built around Liberty's own technology achievements — turning "here's what we do" into something people actually wanted to play.

03

The Result

A first-year sponsorship that felt like it belonged there — one consistent message (Liberty's technology division creates real pathways for Black tech professionals to advance) carried across email, social, on-site screens and a game, rather than a generic booth backdrop and a stack of flyers.
