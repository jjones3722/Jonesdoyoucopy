Client: Urbal Alchemy

Urbal Alchemy

Rewriting a wellness brand's product line, one listing at a time.

ClientUrbal Alchemy
CategoryBrand & Product Copywriting
DeliverableFull product-line copy rewrite
RoleCopywriter
01

The Brief

Urbal Alchemy's owner had built a full line of vegan, CBD-infused chai blends, but the product copy hadn't kept up. It read like placeholder text more than a brand. She asked for a rewrite that better expressed what she was actually selling, so she could put her attention back on running the rest of the business.

02

The Approach

I went listing by listing across the product line (Golden, Masala, Matcha, and Mocha Chai), tightening rambling, inconsistent descriptions into copy that actually sells: clear on the ingredients, clear on the ritual (add hot water, unwind), and consistent from one product page to the next.

03

The Result

A product catalog that reads like one brand instead of four separate afterthoughts, shorter and more scannable, and built around the same benefit-first structure on every page.
