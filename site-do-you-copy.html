Why this is different

Most sites are written after they're designed. Yours won't be.

I've spent years writing the copy other people's websites launch with — brand sites, campaign pages, landing pages for teams who hired a designer first and a writer second, if at all. This site is proof of what happens when the words come first: every headline, every button, every case study on jonesdoyoucopy.com was written and built by one person, end to end. That's what "Site, Do You Copy?" is: the same process, for your business.
