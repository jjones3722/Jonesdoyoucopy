Client: Eaton

Tales From the Next Frontier

The ongoing content platform that grew out of "IT: The Next Frontier" — a running series of campaigns, keeping the same universe alive between big launches.

The idea

One universe, four different moments.

Once "IT: The Next Frontier" launched as a playable game, Eaton kept coming back for more — smaller, faster-turnaround content that could show up for a cultural moment or a specific audience without a full campaign build. Four of those follow-on pieces are below: a recognition campaign, a seasonal push, a wellness series, and a 22-email nurture campaign.

Women in Engineering Day

Recognizing Eaton's own engineers

In honor of Women in Engineering Day, Eaton asked for social posts recognizing the women working in engineering roles across the company — real employees, given real recognition, rather than a generic stock-photo message.

Valentine's Day

Sharing the love, Eaton-style

Static ads and social posts built around a simple seasonal hook — sharing the love — dressed in Eaton's brand voice instead of a generic holiday template.

World Mental Health Day

Meditations, written and voiced by Jay

The brief: make World Mental Health Day content that featured the Eaton brand organically, without a hard sell. The answer was a short series of guided meditations for IT professionals — written and personally voiced, built to offer a real minute of relief rather than another marketing message.

Anteriad Email Campaign

22 emails, three very different audiences

The challenge: help Eaton find inroads with distinct customer segments — data center employees, small business owners, and managers of large enterprises — by showcasing its power management solutions through short, quick-hitting emails rather than one generic blast.
