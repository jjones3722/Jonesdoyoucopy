The idea

One universe, five different moments.

Once "IT: The Next Frontier" launched as a playable game, Eaton kept coming back for more: smaller, faster-turnaround content that could show up for a cultural moment or a specific audience without a full campaign build. Five projects make up the series so far: the original game campaign, a recognition campaign, a seasonal push, a wellness series, and a 22-email nurture campaign. Each has its own case study below.
