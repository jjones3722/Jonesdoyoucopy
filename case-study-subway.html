Client: Subway

In the Sky

A sandwich-wrapped blimp, a public sign-up for free rides, and an activation built to get people talking.

ClientSubway
CategoryExperiential Activation
DeliverableBranded blimp activation, public ride giveaway & ground event
RoleConcept & Execution Lead
01

The Brief

This one isn't a copywriting credit. It's an experiential activation I conceived and led the execution on. Subway wanted a way to get in front of people that didn't feel like an ad: something big, visible and shareable enough to earn attention on its own, rather than buying it.

02

The Approach

I built the idea around a real, working blimp wrapped to look like a giant Subway sandwich: the Subway In the Sky. Instead of keeping it a media stunt, we opened it up to the public: people could apply at Subwayinthesky.com for a shot at an actual ride, turning the activation into something people could win their way into, not just watch. On the ground, a branded photo booth let visitors step into the campaign even if they didn't win a seat.

03

The Result

70,000 social shares of earned media coverage, 13.6 million views, and 359,000 engagements with the branded social content. More than 10,000 people applied at Subwayinthesky.com, and 200 of them won rides. The activation also picked up national press from CNBC, People and Fox Business, plus local coverage from WDAF-TV (Kansas City) and WKMG-TV (Orlando), eight hours of runtime across broadcast.
