Client: Liberty Mutual

Win With Purpose, Together

Rolling out a new company-wide slogan in a way employees around the world would actually notice.

ClientLiberty Mutual
CategoryEmployee Engagement Campaign
DeliverableHQ activation, digital tracker, custom game & recruiting campaign
RoleCopywriter — concepted the Pac-Man game & Medallion Tracker
01

The Brief

Liberty Mutual asked for an engagement campaign to celebrate the launch of its new Enterprise Narrative — and the new company slogan that came with it, "Win with purpose, together." The campaign needed to educate employees on the narrative, inspire them to actually live it, and tie every piece of messaging back to the broader communications framework, for a global workforce, not just headquarters.

02

The Approach

I wrote the copy across the launch and concepted two of the flagship ideas myself: a custom Pac-Man arcade game built into the HQ takeover, and a Global Medallion Tracker — a physical 3D medallion we built and sent traveling around the world to Liberty's offices, tracked on a live site the way people track Santa on Christmas Eve, so every office could watch it coming and get excited for the day it arrived at their door.

03

The Result

A launch employees could actually walk through and play, not just read about: a headquarters redesigned around the new slogan, a tracker that turned a physical object into a global moment, and a custom game on the arcade cabinet in the lobby. The same "Win with purpose, together" platform then carried into recruiting content, the Comparion Insurance sub-brand launch, and an internal webinar.
