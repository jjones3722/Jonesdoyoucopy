Client: GBT Solutions

Great Leaders Are Divergent

Copy that reframes neurodivergence as a leadership advantage, not something to work around.

ClientGBT Solutions
CategoryWebsite Copywriting
DeliverableFull website copy rewrite
RoleCopywriter
The brief

A coaching practice built on a point of view

GBT Solutions needed copy that did more than list coaching, consulting and training services — it needed to clearly articulate a belief the founder holds about how neurodivergent leaders actually succeed: not by being fixed or accommodated, but by being supported in a way that lets them lead from their real strengths.

The approach

Naming the advantage, not the deficit

I wrote the site around a reframe: divergent thinking as a leadership asset, not a limitation to manage around. That shows up from the first line on the homepage, "Great leaders are divergent," through copy that speaks directly to leaders who've been told their brain works differently, and makes the case for why that's exactly what makes them effective.

On the site

Embrace your divergent path

The result

A message people recognize themselves in

The site now gives neurodivergent leaders language for something they've likely felt but rarely heard said back to them this plainly: that real support isn't about lowering the bar, it's what lets them thrive.
