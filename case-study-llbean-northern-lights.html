The brief

A holiday experience, not another seasonal email

Fresh, family-friendly copy for L.L.Bean's annual festival of lights: signage, landing pages, warming benches, brochures, holiday kiosks and more, all pointed at one goal: driving attendance.

The work

Northern Lights

Campaign key art, on-site signage and a trifold event brochure, all built around an illustrated holiday village: string lights, a snow globe display, a horse-drawn sleigh, the kind of scene that reads as an invitation rather than an ad.

Print

The event brochure

A trifold brochure covering the full festival: opening night and tree lighting, daily activities, and the ongoing attractions running throughout the season.

Signage

Warming bench copy: three options, two winners

Wrote three headline options for the park's sponsored warming benches. They chose two: "The Hottest Seats in Town" and "Share the Warmth."
