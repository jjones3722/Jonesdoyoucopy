Client: Eaton

IT: The Next Frontier

Turning a lineup of IT products into a playable space adventure.

ClientEaton
CategoryContent & Social Campaign
DeliverablePlayable mobile game copy + social ad campaign
RoleCopywriter — Game & Social Copy
01

The Brief

Present Eaton's many IT solutions in a fun, out-of-this-world way that would get people talking, clicking and converting into Eaton customers — with an idea that actually showed how Eaton makes the everyday work lives of its users easier, not just another feature list.

02

The Approach

The idea we landed on: a playable mobile game, IT: The Next Frontier, where the player is stranded in space and has to solve puzzles and repair a broken ship to make it back to Earth — a stand-in for exactly the kind of IT chaos Eaton's products are built to prevent. I wrote most of the in-game copy, from the mission setup to the villains (a scheming Devil Duck and a server-snatching alien), plus the social posts and Facebook ad copy that drove people to play.

03

The Result

A full campaign built around one story instead of a scattered product pitch — game copy, ad copy and creative all pointing back to the same space-adventure hook, turning a comprehensive IT portfolio into something people wanted to open and play rather than skim past.
